ODEs · Laplace · PDEs
Differential Equations · 2026–2027

Course Objectives

The objectives of this course are to introduce students to the concept of a differential equation, basic techniques for solving certain classes of differential equations, especially linear equations, and connections between qualitative features of equations and their solutions. Connections to problems from the physical world are emphasized. In addition to ordinary differential equations, the course introduces selected partial differential equations.

Course Content

First-order equations and applications; higher-order linear differential equations; power-series solutions; Laplace transform and initial-value problems; systems of linear differential equations; introduction to partial differential equations.

Course Learning Outcomes

  • Classify and identify different types of differential equations.
  • Solve important classes of ordinary differential equations and interpret their qualitative behaviour.
  • Apply linear algebra to solve linear ODEs and systems.
  • Model physical phenomena using differential equations and interpret solutions physically.
  • Use power-series methods for second-order linear differential equations.
  • Apply the Laplace transform to differential equations.
  • Use separation of variables and Fourier series for basic partial differential equations.

Biography

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Hacettepe University. My research focuses on the mathematical and computational modeling of complex dynamical systems, with particular emphasis on stochastic processes, differential equations, and data-driven computational approaches.

My work integrates deterministic and stochastic modeling with numerical methods, Markov jump processes, stochastic differential equations, Monte Carlo Markov chain methods, and neural networks. My main application areas include biochemical reaction systems, signalling pathways, and epidemic models.

More recently, I have extended these methodologies to the stochastic modeling of malware propagation and cyber attacks. Across these application areas, my research aims to develop mathematically rigorous and computationally efficient methods for the analysis, simulation, inference, and prediction of complex stochastic systems.
